## Full post

=== START ===
MileClear is on Android

The Android app went into Google Play closed testing today and I need testers. If you have an Android phone and drive for work, this is the one to try.

What it is: the same app as the iPhone version. Automatic trip recording, manual trips, shifts, HMRC mileage rates (55p and 25p, 24p for motorbikes), fuel prices, earnings and expenses, the Self Assessment wizard, and Google Sign-In.

What to expect: it is a first build on a new platform, so it will say "basic tracking" (the recording engine used on iPhone needs a licence I have not bought for Android yet) and Pro cannot be bought in it yet. If you want Pro to test, message me and I will switch it on for you. Live Activities, Siri and CarPlay are Apple-only, so they are not there.

How to join: open this link on your phone, opt in, then install from Google Play.
[JOIN LINK]

Google needs 12 testers to keep the app installed for 14 days before it can go live, so even keeping it on your phone helps. The most useful thing after that is one real drive with the app closed, and a note on whether the trip appeared.

Feedback: support@mileclear.com or the Feedback screen in the app. For a missed drive, use "Missing a trip you made?" in the app, because it sends the diagnostic I fix from.
=== END ===
