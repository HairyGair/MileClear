## 2. Tue 1 Sep: 1.3.8 on iPhone, what changed (image: sep-02-ios-138.png)
Nine capture fixes shipped in 1.3.8, and every one came from a driver telling us a drive went wrong.

Short hops under a mile between two stops used to be thrown away. Now every leg is its own trip. Start Trip and forget to tap Arrived? It still saves. Killed the app mid-drive? It fills the gap and tells you which miles were estimated. And you can now choose how long a stop has to be before it ends a journey (Settings, Tracking).

If a drive ever goes missing, use "Missing a trip you made?" in the app. The diagnostic it sends is what we fix from.

## 3. Fri 4 Sep: HMRC 55p (image: sep-03-hmrc-55p.png)
The HMRC mileage rate for cars and vans went up to 55p a mile on 6 April 2026, for the first 10,000 business miles in the tax year, then 25p. Motorbikes stay at 24p.

10,000 business miles is now worth £5,500 of tax relief. MileClear applies the right rate by trip date, so older trips stay at 45p and this year's are at 55p, with nothing to change.

Full rates and worked examples: mileclear.com/hmrc-mileage-rates

## 4. Tue 8 Sep: dead miles (image: sep-04-dead-miles.png)
The miles between jobs count.

Driving from one drop to the next pickup, heading back into town after a delivery, repositioning while you wait for the next ping: none of it is paid by the platform, and all of it is business mileage HMRC recognises.

Most drivers only log the paid legs. Automatic recording captures the lot, which is usually the difference between a small claim and a real one.

## 5. Fri 11 Sep: fuel prices (image: sep-05-fuel.png)
Every UK fuel station now has to publish its prices, and MileClear reads the government feed: 8,300+ stations, live.

Open the Fuel tab, see what is cheapest near you, log the fill-up and it lands in your running costs. Free.

## 6. Tue 15 Sep: Self Assessment countdown (image: sep-06-sa-countdown.png)
138 days until the Self Assessment deadline on 31 January.

The Tax Readiness card in MileClear shows what you are likely to owe from what you have driven and earned so far, suggests a weekly amount to set aside, and counts down to the deadline. It updates every time a trip lands. Free, on the dashboard.
