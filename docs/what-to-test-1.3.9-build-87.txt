Build 87 is OPEN and accumulating. Add to this list as work lands, not when the
build is cut.

Everything build 86 asked for still stands, because 86 has not been driven yet.
Items 1 to 3 are the reason 85 was replaced: its stop fix did not work.

1. DRIVE, STOP SOMEWHERE FOR TEN MINUTES OR MORE, THEN DRIVE ON
   Two trips, not one. Get out and walk about during the stop rather than
   sitting in the car: a carried phone is exactly what the old code could never
   read as a stop, because the engine refreshed its own idle clock on every GPS
   fix including the ones from a parked phone.

2. STOP FOR FIVE MINUTES SOMEWHERE WITH POOR SIGNAL, THEN DRIVE ON
   Also two trips. The old rule asked how far the phone had moved by the time it
   woke up, which is the wrong question when it wakes half a mile down the road.

3. DRIVE THROUGH A TUNNEL OR A LONG BLACKSPOT WITHOUT STOPPING
   The regression check: this must stay ONE trip.

4. OPEN A FINISHED TRIP AND LOOK ABOVE THE BUSINESS/PERSONAL BUTTONS
   A map of the route. This was in build 85 and never appeared for anyone: it
   read a local table nothing writes to. It now comes from the server, so it
   also works for trips recorded on another device.

5. CORRECT WHERE A JOURNEY STARTED  (new in 87)
   Open a tracked trip, tap Start Location, move it back to where you actually
   set off, save. Check three things:
     - the start address changes to the place you picked;
     - the distance goes UP by roughly the road distance you added, and the
       route on the map keeps everything it recorded;
     - the trip does not appear twice in the list afterwards.
   Then try it on a manual trip: there is no recorded route, so the distance is
   re-worked between the two ends instead.
   Finally, drag the pin somewhere absurd, tens of miles away. The label should
   move and the mileage should NOT: we will not price a stretch that far.

6. A SHORT HOP THAT USED TO VANISH  (new in 87)
   Drive somewhere under half a mile at normal road speed, park, and wait a
   minute. The app will still decide the recording is too short to save on its
   own, but it should now appear under Missed Journeys saying "We recorded this
   one but it was too short to save on its own", at the ROAD distance rather
   than the straight line. Add it and check the mileage looks right.
   Then do a there-and-back: out to somewhere close and straight back to where
   you started. That one used to be invisible twice over, because returning to
   your starting point leaves no gap for the app to notice.
   The opposite check matters too: shuffling the car around a car park must
   still produce nothing at all.

7. PARK AND CLASSIFY FROM THE DYNAMIC ISLAND
   Untested since build 85. Business and Personal should both be offered, and
   the choice should stick when you next open the app.

8. WATCH THE LIVE ACTIVITY TIMER
   Untested since build 85. It must count from today, not from 2057.

9. A SHORT HOP OF UNDER A MINUTE
   Should say it was too short to record, on the lock screen, rather than
   silently disappearing.

Anything that looks like a journey you did not make, or one of yours that is
missing, is worth reporting however small it seems.
