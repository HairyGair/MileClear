Build 86 replaces build 85. Same version, because 1.3.9 has not gone for review.

Everything build 85 asked for still needs doing, and three of the items below
are the reason it is being replaced: build 85's stop fix did not actually work.

1. DRIVE, STOP SOMEWHERE FOR TEN MINUTES OR MORE, THEN DRIVE ON
   This is the main one. You should get two trips, not one. Build 85 gave one:
   the ten-minute stop timeout could never fire, because the engine refreshed
   its own idle clock on every GPS fix including the ones from a parked phone.
   Getting out and walking about during the stop is the case that was broken,
   so please do that rather than sitting in the car.

2. STOP FOR FIVE MINUTES SOMEWHERE WITH POOR SIGNAL, THEN DRIVE ON
   Also two trips. The old rule asked how far the phone had moved by the time
   it woke up again, which is the wrong question when it wakes half a mile down
   the road. It now asks whether the car had stopped before the phone went
   quiet.

3. DRIVE THROUGH A TUNNEL OR A LONG SIGNAL BLACKSPOT WITHOUT STOPPING
   The opposite check, and the one to watch for regressions: this must stay ONE
   trip. A four-minute silence at speed is a tunnel, not a visit.

4. OPEN A FINISHED TRIP AND LOOK ABOVE THE BUSINESS/PERSONAL BUTTONS
   There should be a map of the route. This was in build 85 and never appeared
   for anybody: it was reading a local table that nothing ever writes to. It now
   comes from the server, so it also works for trips recorded on another device.

5. PARK AT THE END OF A DRIVE AND CLASSIFY FROM THE DYNAMIC ISLAND
   Unchanged from build 85 and still untested on a phone: Business and Personal
   should both be there, and the choice should stick when you open the app.

6. WATCH THE LIVE ACTIVITY TIMER
   Unchanged from build 85 and still untested on a phone: it must count from
   today, not from the year 2057.

7. A SHORT HOP OF UNDER A MINUTE
   Should say it was too short to record, on the lock screen, rather than
   silently disappearing.

Anything that looks like a journey you did not make, or a journey of yours that
is missing, is worth reporting even if it seems small.
