## LinkedIn (Anthony's profile)

MileClear drivers passed two million miles on Monday night.

The first million took seven months. The second took five weeks.

Between them, 1,603 drivers have recorded 176,910 journeys, and 96% of those recorded themselves. Nobody pressed start. Just under half the miles were for work: 940,742 business miles, close to half a million pounds of mileage claims at HMRC's rates, each one backed by a dated log instead of a guess at the end of the year.

Before posting this I went through the trips that should not count. A phone that wakes up a few times on a plane can produce a thousand-mile "drive" at 500 mph, so flights are out. Going the other way, I found 217 real drives that a bug had hidden, and put them back. The two million is drives only.

I build MileClear on my own, and nearly every improvement this year started with a driver emailing to say something was wrong. Thank you to every one of them.

The full numbers are in the first comment.
