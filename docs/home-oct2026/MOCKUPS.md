# Home redesign: mockups (10 Oct 2026)

iPhone 17 size (402 x 874 pt), drawn with the tokens in `SPEC-VISUAL.md` for the layouts in `PROPOSALS.md`. Each is an SVG plus a 2x PNG to look at (`mockups/*.png`). Figures are illustrative demo numbers for a gig driver mid-October (2,517 business miles since 6 April at 55p = £1,384.35). Start Shift is drawn as the secondary button (SPEC-VISUAL 5.4, open point).

## The three layouts (Work mode, all fine)

- `mockups/a-work-fine` - **Layout A, calm list (recommended):** status line, claim, Start Trip + Start Shift, last trip, three door rows, one ask; all on one screen.
- `mockups/b-work-fine` - **Layout B, buttons first, tiles:** Start Trip at the top, last trip, two figure tiles, four door tiles (the claim shrinks to a tile).
- `mockups/c-work-fine` - **Layout C, one card that changes:** a large moment card (here its "otherwise" state: claim plus progress to 10,000 miles), buttons, doors with last trip as a door.

## Layout A, other states

- `mockups/a-personal-fine` - Personal mode: October miles lead, one full-width Start Trip, today's trip already Personal, doors to Insights, Badges and Fuel.
- `mockups/a-work-something-wrong` - Background location lost: the status line becomes the red "Trips aren't recording · Fix" strip, the ask row is hidden, nothing else moves.
- `mockups/a-work-just-finished` - A drive just saved: "+£6.82 from your last trip" by the claim, the last trip card open with Business pre-set (Auto) and "2 more trips to sort".
- `mockups/a-work-new-driver` - No trips yet: amber "Finish setting up" status, no hero (no £0.00), Start Trip full width, "Your first trip" card, one door, one ask.
